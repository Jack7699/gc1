About GRAND CITY 1

GRAND CITY 1 (GC1) is the first chapter of the GRAND CITY franchise: an original open-world crime-action game set in a fictional coastal metropolis inspired by California.

This prototype lets you walk and drive across a large city, discover districts, and play the opening storyline of Silas Brenn, Lena Morrow and Victor Hale.

All characters, vehicles and places are original fiction.
